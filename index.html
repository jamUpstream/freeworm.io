Ready to play
Controls
Mouse / Touch: move cursor / drag to steer.
Hold click / touch: Boost (costs mass).
W or Space: Boost (keyboard).
Objective
Eat pellets to grow. Avoid crashing your head into any worm's body — including your own.
Cut off other worms to make them crash into you.
Power-ups
Shield: blocks one fatal hit (but you still take a Score Penalty when hit).
Magnet: pulls nearby pellets toward you.
Haste: temporary speed boost.
2×: double mass gain for a short time.
Zoom: Gain temporary zoom-out ability.
Bomb: Score, Mass and Size Penalty when pick up.
